## 6. Rank promotion & maintenance (criteria)

**Star Manager (SM):** achieve personal sales of 30 **or** group sales of 50
units net sales within 3 months; recruit minimum 5 new SAs (1st level) during the
qualification month; attend Pre‑STAR Manager training; attend management
interview (promotion subject to management approval).

**Star Chief Manager (SCM):** minimum **600 units** net sales in personal or
alliance group sales within 3 months; promote minimum 4 SMs (1st level);
training + management interview.

**Star Elite Officer (SEO):**
- Option A: minimum **10,000 units** net sales (personal/alliance) within 6
  months & 4 SCMs (1st level).
- Option B: minimum **5,000 units** net sales within 3 months + promote minimum 4
  SCMs (1st level). For Option B, each SCM group must achieve minimum group sales
  of **400 units net sales/month**.

**SM maintenance:** minimum accumulative sales of **45 units per quarter**
(effective Q2 2023 onwards); conduct weekly meeting with SA; attend company
events/training. Status reviewed quarterly (worked example: 15+15+15=SM; 15+10+25=SM;
10+10+20=SA).

## 8. Data model implications (for the incentive module)

Minimum data needed to compute/monitor incentives per SA:

- **SA identity** and role (SA / SM / SCM / SEO).
- **Sales records**: product, category, MOVON vs CUCKOO, plan type
  (outright/rental), Duo pairing, promotion flags (RM12, Samsung, Joy Pack),
  net/install status, date.
- **Derived unit count** per the applicable rule set.
- **Collection records** for rental collection commission eligibility (and
  2‑month arrears rule).
- **Period** (month/quarter) for periodic incentive and SBI thresholds.

The module should store the **raw facts** (sales/leads/collections) and compute
amounts in a pure, unit-tested function — never store a hard-coded rate in the UI
and never expose amounts outside the owner/admin scope.

## 9. Security requirements (hard)

1. All reads/writes require an authenticated session; the record's `advisor_id`
   must equal the session's `advisorId` (admins excepted).
2. Enforce at **both** the server action/route layer and Supabase **RLS**.
3. Never return another SA's rows via manipulated IDs, direct URLs, query params
   or server actions.
4. No public caching / no `revalidate` of personalized pages; responses must not
   be indexable (`robots: noindex`).
5. Audit access to incentive data, but audit metadata must not contain PII or
   amounts.
6. Never ship the service-role key or raw rate tables to the client.
