## 4. Product information extracted

- **Outright prices** (normal and promotional), with savings where stated.
- **Rent-to-own plans**: plan label, monthly price, tenure and obligation period.
- **Warranty**: product/motor/compressor terms where documented (e.g. LockMate
  2yr; MOVON laundry/fridge 1yr product + 10yr motor/compressor; MOVON Baby 3yr).
- **Promotion mechanics**: RM1 + 50% off / RM1 OR 50% off; RPF RM250 waived;
  15%/20% outright with free gift or extended warranty; combo bundles.
- **Service plans (CCSP)**: 1 / 1+1 / 1+2 / 2 / 2+1 years where listed.
- **Free gifts / remarks**: free timer, mattress protectors, massage gun, bed
  covers, free installation and FOC cards (LockMate).

## 5. Missing / conflicting information (flagged, not invented)

1. **No technical specifications** (dimensions, capacity beyond what is stated,
   power, materials) in the promotion PDFs for most products.
2. **No installation requirements** documented for any product.
3. **No FAQs** are present in any of the four documents.
4. **"For more info, please refer to MEMO"** appears on every promotional page —
   final terms are governed by a memo not supplied.
5. **Product images** are not supplied; new draft records have no `imageUrl`.
6. **Combo B labelling**: the source detail page labels the discounted row
   "COOLMATE + WASHMATE" while the combo is CoolMate + DuoMate (RM4,999).
7. **Effective-date typo**: one MOVON image page shows "1 Apr – 30 Jun 2026"
   while the rest of the document uses Q4 2026 dates.
8. **Air-conditioner branding**: the MOVON Q4 rental section shows CHiQ branding
   while the aircond category is otherwise "MOVON SnowMate"; the CKI document
   lists a separate "VITA 5TAR" aircon.
9. **ChillMate 508L / SnowMate / Car Seat / Care** are not covered by either
   promotion document, so no pricing could be updated.
10. **A LUXE / A LITE** have many size/frame permutations; only representative
    rental and outright figures were recorded.
11. **Rental figures** are the current promotional prices ("RM1 + 50% off"),
    not the standard/default rental price; some documents list a "Rental Default
    Price" that differs.
12. **BM copy**: source documents are English; the catalogue schema is
    single-language (no per-locale product fields), so BM product copy cannot be
    added without a schema change. Only the "Bayar Ringan Je" campaign name is
    Malay.
